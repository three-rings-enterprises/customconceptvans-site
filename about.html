From Humble Beginnings

Founder, Joseph P. - "My first build was a labor of love, pictured here under the wing of the Twin Otter aircraft used for hundreds of thousands of skydives for anything from tandem jumps to world records. Like so many, I was driven to van life through the pursuit of my passions - skydiving, snowboarding, rock climbing, and the great outdoors. I wanted a way to get closer to what I loved without restrictions. From there, a journey began which took me all across the country, from deep powder stashes to military skydive training  facilities, and every rock wall in between..."

Our Mission

Our goal is to give you the freedom to explore the world the way you desire, and get you closer to your passions and the people you share them with. 

Our Mindset

Our approach is safety, functionality and sustainability, with customer driven designs using locally sourced materials. 

Goodbye Status Quo

"...With a background in architecture, electrical and construction project management, designing and building custom van conversions was a logical transition for me. After three years living full time in my creation and immersing into the community of van lifers, a deeper understanding of what makes a build functional was formed. Today, I offer that knowledge and experience to you to cultivate your vision and bring your dreams to life, to get you into the world and on whatever adventure calls your name."
