Window Installation Process

Window Up Your Van

Custom Concept Vans offers professional, warrantied installation of your new van windows. We not only cut in new windows, but we can also perform the interior finishing needed to make everything look factory. 

How does it work?

First select the windows you'd like to add to your van. Once special order windows are in stock we will schedule your van for installation.


Windows are installed in a climate controlled environment with an end result that looks factory. Windows take 1-2 days to install depending on how many are required. There is a minimum 6 hours safe drive away time for glued in windows, so in general expect to have the van here overnight.

What windows can I choose from?

Visit our partner website, below, to choose your windows. Once you select your windows, send us the links to all windows desired and we will provide the discounted price, along with installation price and timeline.


We also offer Arctic Tern and other windows that are not available through VWD. If you'd like something else you don't see there, contact us for more information.

Choose Windows

How much does it cost?

Installation is $450 per large window and $350 per small window (rear doors and bunk windows). This price includes only installing the windows in empty cargo models. 


Vans with existing equipment requiring additional work to perform a window install are assessed on a case by case basis. 


Interior panelling prep and finishing is not included. For price please inquire with more details.

Is there a warranty?

We offer a 1 year warranty on workmanship and all body penetrations are prepped with a rust inhibiting primer for long lasting quality.
