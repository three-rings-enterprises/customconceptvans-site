Claim jumper - camper van conversion

Meet the Claim Jumper, a flexible modern camper van conversion ready to move from adventure to adventure, and everything in between. This Mercedes Sprinter 144 was commissioned for an ultra light build to make the most of it's efficient 4 cylinder diesel engine. Extruded aluminum cabinetry helps keeps the weight down, weighing in at 2000 lbs below GVWR. Flarespace flares allow for more open floor space, and the hinged mattress and removable platform panels make it easy to convert this van from a mini hotel to a flexible gear hauler, with plenty of tie down capacity.

build specs

Exterior

  • Flarespace flares with bunk windows
  • T-vent crew window @ drivers forward section, solid glass window @ slider door, solid glass windows in rear doors 
  • Backwoods Adventure Mods Driftr roof rack and ladder combo
  • Black Rhino Arsenal Wheels w/ Falken Wildpeak 265/70R17 Tires
  • Steelcraft heavy duty side steps


Interior

  • 3 zones of dimmable lighting, plus motion activated interior cabinet lights 
  • Upholstered full interior paneling and headliner shelf over drivers seats
  • Marine plywood floor with coin cell vinyl glued down
  • Fully insulated with 3M Thinsulate throughout and minicell foam under floor
  • L-Track recessed in floor @ rear half


Comfort

  • Swivel seats on drivers and passengers seats
  • Diesel heater w/auto high altitude mode
  • Maxxair roof vent fan above bed, with insulated interior cover
  • Dometic CRX 110L fridge has ample space for long journeys
  • Portable dual induction cooktop
  • Hot water sink and outdoor shower
  • Electric cassette toilet 


Electrical

  • Battle Born lithium battery bank, expandable to 400ah
  • Victron charging components, including battery monitor, Orion 360w alternator charger, MPPT 100/30 solar charge controller
  • Xantrex Freedom XC 2000w Inverter Charger w/ shore power connection
  • 2000w Inverter powers 4 interior 120v outlets
  • Thermally controlled electrical enclosure maximizes charger output
  • Pre-wired for forward vent fan 
  • Prewired for rear door speakers
  • 12v power and USB charger outlets throughout van


Plumbing

  • 20 Gallon fresh water tank mounted inside with 12v pump
  • 10 gallon undermount grey water tank with elecronic dump valve
  • 4 Gallon Isotemp water heater undermounted, heats via engine coolant and 120v electric
  • Ruvati 15 x 15 interior sink  with cutting board and drinking water filtration
  • Wash down sprayer/outdoor shower @ rear doors
  • Thetford Curve 565E portapotti


Cabinetry

  • Ultralight cabinetry framed with 1 in. x 1 in. 8020 aluminum extrusions 
  • Lightweight cabinet skins sprayed with CCV signature blue enamel paint
  • Amber edge grain 3/4" bamboo plywood doors and faces, hand rubbed with OsmoPolyx
  • Solid Acacia butcher block countertop
  • Wheel well cabinets lined with durable coin cell vinyl in garage area
  • Two double door overhead cabinets with soft close lift hardware and motion interior lighting
  • Galley cabinet with plenty of storage and front loading fridge 
  • Slam latch hardware keep all compartments secure while driving
  • Lagun swivel table for drivers and passengers swivel seats
  • Bench seat with portapotti compartment, storage compartment, and slide-out outside table  
  • Removable bed platform system with custom hinged flarespace mattress
  • Dometic CRX 110L fridge with ample space for long journeys
  • Portable induction cookt


The Claim Jumper is a flexible build for a wide range of users. This full build can be remade as is or customized for your liking. If you are interested in having this build completed in your van, contact today for more information.
